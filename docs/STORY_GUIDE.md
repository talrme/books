# Family story guide

## Established facts

- **Miri**: four-year-old protagonist. The parent supplied a family photo after the first drafts: light hair worn in braids. Use that appearance for future illustration work. The initial published pictures use an earlier invented appearance with dark curls and have not yet been updated.
- **Abba**: her dad. In the supplied reference: wavy brown hair and a short beard.
- **Mama**: her mom. In the supplied reference: brown hair pulled back. Her hat and sunglasses belong to that photo and are not mandatory in every scene.
- **Avi**: Miri’s infant baby brother; the homecoming story depicts him as a newborn. She affectionately calls him **Rainbow Baby**. Do not add a medical history or explanation for that nickname.
- **Ayla and Asher**: Miri’s cousins and swimming-lesson companions.
- **YMCA**: the destination for swimming lessons.
- The family car is a **Tesla Model Y**, nicknamed **the Spaceship**. Flying in space is explicitly Miri’s imagination.
- Miri has a **blue bike**.
- The family loves dancing together and eating together around the table.

## Tone and structure

Warm read-aloud English, ages about four. Short concrete sentences, gentle repetition, child-sized wonder, and a reassuring ending. A little fear is okay; do not imply that bravery requires having no fear. Favor loving family moments over a lesson spelled out to the reader. Keep depictions of swimming and cycling age-appropriate without inserting warnings into the story.

## The first three books

1. **Miri Makes a Splash** — watercolor. A ride in the rear seat of Abba’s bicycle, a YMCA lesson with Ayla and Asher, a nervous start, bubbles, kicking, floating, dreams of bigger swims and pool parties, and a warm towel hug. Page turns use a ripple reveal.
2. **Avi, Our Rainbow Baby** — paper collage. Waiting at the hospital, Avi coming home, a tiny hand, a family dance, the Model Y/Spaceship, an imaginary space ride, dinner, and a big-sister goodnight. Page turns use paper flutter.
3. **Miri’s Very Big Little Adventure** — gouache and colored pencil. A homemade map, Miri’s blue bike, a snail, a passing shower, a rainbow, a hilltop picnic, the ride home, and a story to tell Mama. Page turns use a dimensional turn.

These are first drafts for the parent to review. Artwork varies by book style; character likeness is broadly consistent rather than a locked portrait. Original exact image-generation prompts are in `art-prompts.json`.

## Family reference and editorial plans

The parent identified the people in the supplied photograph as Abba, Miri, Mama, and Avi. Use it as a private reference when available; the public repository contains written illustration notes only. Do not infer unseen details or require everyone to wear their photo outfits. Preserve recognizable features across the different book styles. If a later illustration task needs the original reference and it is unavailable, ask for it again.
