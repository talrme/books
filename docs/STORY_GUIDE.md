# Family story guide

## Established facts

- **Miri**: four-year-old protagonist. The parent supplied a family photo after the first drafts: light hair worn in braids. Use that appearance for future illustration work. Both upper front teeth are currently missing; include that gap in her smile. The illustrations have been regenerated from the family reference.
- **Abba**: her dad. In the supplied reference: wavy brown hair and a short beard.
- **Mama**: her mom. In the supplied reference: brown hair pulled back. Her hat and sunglasses belong to that photo and are not mandatory in every scene.
- **Avi**: Miri’s infant baby brother; the homecoming story depicts him as a newborn. She affectionately calls him **Rainbow Baby**. Do not add a medical history or explanation for that nickname.
- **Ayla and Asher**: Miri’s cousins and swimming-lesson companions.
- **YMCA**: the destination for swimming lessons.
- The family car is a **Tesla Model Y**, nicknamed **the Spaceship**. Flying in space is explicitly Miri’s imagination.
- Miri has a **blue bike**.
- The family loves dancing together and eating together around the table.

## Tone and structure

Warm read-aloud English, ages about four. Short concrete sentences, gentle repetition, child-sized wonder, and a reassuring ending. A little fear is okay; do not imply that bravery requires having no fear. Favor loving family moments over a lesson spelled out to the reader. Keep depictions of swimming and cycling age-appropriate without inserting warnings into the story.

## The books

1. **Miri Makes a Splash** — watercolor. A ride in the rear seat of Abba’s bicycle, a YMCA lesson with Ayla and Asher, a nervous start, bubbles, kicking, floating, dreams of bigger swims and pool parties, and a warm towel hug. Page turns use the shared dimensional flip.
2. **Avi, Our Rainbow Baby** — paper collage. Waiting at the hospital, Avi coming home, a tiny hand, a family dance, the Model Y/Spaceship, an imaginary space ride, dinner, and a big-sister goodnight. Page turns use the shared dimensional flip.
3. **Miri’s Very Big Little Adventure** — gouache and colored pencil. A homemade map, Miri’s blue bike, a snail, a passing shower, a rainbow, a hilltop picnic, the ride home, and a story to tell Mama. Page turns use the shared dimensional flip.

4. **Rainbow Baby Sings!** — dreamy watercolor, twelve story pages. Avi stays an infant in rainbow clothing. Singing in rain and sunshine leads to a make-believe unicorn ride over the rainbow and a school show with Miri, Ayla and Asher. Mama, Abba and other adults are the audience. Everyone cheers for Rainbow Baby on page 12.

These books remain open for the parent to review. Artwork varies by book style; character likeness is broadly consistent rather than a locked portrait. Original exact image-generation prompts are in `art-prompts.json`; the current 27 individual family-reference illustrations use `art-prompts-family.json`.
