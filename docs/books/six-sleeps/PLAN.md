# Six Sleeps Until Cousin Hugs

- Book ID: six-sleeps
- Stage: published
- Current revision: 3 — November 15, 2026
- Audience and language: age four, English, read together
- Story brief: Miri counts six sleeps until Levi, Matan, Aunt Taylor and Uncle Lior visit from Minnesota for Thanksgiving week. Bedtime dreams anticipate the adventures; real arrival closes the book.
- Feeling and things to avoid: excited, loving, playful; gentle bathroom humor with no gross imagery. No implying the dream adventures already happened.
- Illustration style and character references: Approved: dreamy luminous watercolor and pencil, inspired by the existing Avi Spaceship illustration, with golden autumn colors, soft lilac dream edges on pages 2–12, and clearer edges for waking scenes. Family likeness follows the private reference photos. Miri has blonde braids and both upper front teeth missing; Avi remains an infant, in rainbow gear. Levi is the older, taller cousin with short brown hair; Matan is the younger, smaller cousin with dark hair. Use the supplied photos for Lior, Taylor, Abba and Mama. Ayla follows her established dark-haired ponytail appearance.
- Motion: existing shared page turn, including portrait-phone full leaf; no new effects.
- Proposed story-page count: 16, excluding covers.
- Pacing recommendation: sixteen short pages give each activity room, separate the dream from the real countdown, and leave a full page for arrival hugs.
- Publication scope: parent approved completing, illustrating and publishing the book on November 15, 2026, with later edits if needed. Revision 3 changes page 13's words and illustrations on pages 4, 7 and 13; all other words and page order remain unchanged.
- Reader: https://talrme.github.io/books/#book=six-sleeps

The royal AI image is a specifically requested future creative asset, incorporated into the page 8 creative illustration; the other original reference photographs remain private.
- Name checks: parent confirmed Ayla and Uncle Lior on November 15, 2026.
- Inclusion assumption: Ayla and infant Avi appear in every dream scene; Avi is safely held, carried, or in a stroller, never doing gymnastics, handling tools or riding a bike. This follows the final instruction to include him despite the interrupted dictation.
- Visual direction approved November 15: dreamy watercolor, with the magical color and tactile quality of Avi’s Spaceship page. Original supplied royal AI portrait is incorporated on page 8; its pink outfit is a deliberate exception to Avi’s otherwise rainbow clothing.
